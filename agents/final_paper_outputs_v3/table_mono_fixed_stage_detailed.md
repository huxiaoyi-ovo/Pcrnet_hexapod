## Evaluation contract

- One Strong Mono training seed (seed 1); three independent evaluation seeds (101/102/103).
- Deterministic policy; 128 episodes per seed; 64 parallel environments.
- Difficulty levels: 0.0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, and 1.0.
- Each complete aggregate cell contains 384 episodes. Uncertainty is sample SD across the three evaluation seeds.
- Strict task success is the recorded task_success_rate: final row crossed, target inside the follow band, and no episode collision.
- Full-row success is the recorded full_task_success_rate: row progress equals 1 and no episode collision; it does not require the final follow-band condition.
- Rates in the CSV are stored as fractions in [0,1]; the Markdown tables display percentages.
- NE means not evaluated, not zero.
- Frozen checkpoint set: 1000, 2000, 2500, 3000, 4000, and 5000. The available 1500 diagnostic point is intentionally excluded from the final checkpoint set.
